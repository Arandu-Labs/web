2. CATEGORIES OF DATA COLLECTED

We process data based on the principle of data minimization. We only collect and process information that is strictly necessary for technical operation, security, and improvement of our Services.

2.1 Automatic Collection via Advertising SDK Infrastructure

To ensure accessibility and keep our Services free (such as Prisma Noticias and free tiers of FirmApp), Arandu Labs LLC integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and third-party mediation platforms. By default and inherent to their technical operation, these systems collect, encrypt in transit (via TLS/HTTPS), and automatically share with Google and authorized partners the following telemetry data points for ad delivery, behavioral analytics, and systemic fraud prevention:

  • IP Addresses: Invariably collected to estimate the device's general geographic location (country/city granularity), measure ad performance, and, subject to applicable user consent, orchestrate ad campaign personalization.
  • User Interaction Metrics: Collection of user interface events, including app launch/exit logs, touch interaction coordinates (taps) on advertising elements, and video view retention metrics.
  • Diagnostic & System Telemetry: Extraction of data related to app performance and third-party SDK stability, including load latencies, interface hang rates, and device energy consumption footprints, used in aggregate for bug mitigation.
  • Unique Device and Account Identifiers: Systematic extraction of the Android Advertising ID (AAID), the iOS Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), the App Set ID structured for developer ecosystem tracking, and, where applicable, cryptographic session identifiers for accounts signed in on the device.

Precise Location Data

Location Data Collection: Some of our Services may collect precise geographic location information (such as GPS, Wi-Fi, or cell tower data) only when you explicitly grant permission through your device settings.

Purposes: If you grant permission, precise location data may be used for ad personalization, analytics, and attribution by our advertising partners. All location data is transmitted via encrypted channels.

2.2 Usage Data & Performance Metrics

To maintain the stability and quality of our Services, we collect anonymous metrics through third-party SDKs, specifically Firebase:

  • Technical Logs: Detailed crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) to identify and fix bugs.
  • Interaction Metrics: Frequency of use, features accessed, and session duration.
  • Performance Events: Application load times and resource utilization.

2.3 Local Content & Zero-Knowledge Architecture

CRITICAL PRIVACY DISCLOSURE: For applications like FirmApp and financial tools, Arandu Labs LLC employs a "Zero-Knowledge" local architecture.

Your files (PDFs, images, documents, financial records, or signatures) are processed LOCALLY ON YOUR DEVICE. Arandu Labs LLC does not upload, store, or have access to your personal documents on our servers. The processing occurs within the sandbox of your device, ensuring maximum confidentiality.

3. COOKIES AND TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES

Arandu Labs LLC and our advertising technology partners use various technologies to collect and process information as part of our Services. These technologies include:

  • Cookies: Small text files stored on your device that help us recognize you and remember your preferences.
  • Web Beacons: Small electronic images embedded in web pages and emails that allow us to track usage and deliver content.
  • IP Addresses: Numerical identifiers assigned to your device when connecting to the internet, used for analytics, security, ad serving, and performance measurement.
  • Device Identifiers: Unique identifiers such as Advertising IDs (IDFA for iOS, AAID for Android) used for advertising and analytics.
  • SDKs: Software Development Kits integrated into our mobile applications to enable third-party services.

7. PERSONALIZED ADVERTISING

Arandu Labs LLC and our advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers, and other technologies to deliver personalized advertisements based on your interests and browsing behavior. This practice is commonly known as "interest-based advertising" or "behavioral advertising".

AdChoices Icon: When you see personalized advertisements, you may notice an "AdChoices" icon (typically a small triangle with the letters "Ad" or "i"). This icon indicates that the ad is personalized based on your interests. Clicking on this icon will provide you with information about the advertising and options to opt out.

Opt-Out Options: You have the right to opt out of personalized advertising. You can exercise this right through:

  • Google Ads Settings: You can opt out of personalized advertising served by Google by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Device Settings: On mobile devices, you can limit ad tracking through system settings (e.g., "Limit Ad Tracking" on iOS or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android).
  • Browser Settings: On web browsers, you can manage cookie preferences and use browser extensions that block third-party cookies.
  • Industry Programs: You can opt out of interest-based advertising from participating companies through programs such as the Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) or the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI), or by visiting AboutAds Choices.

Please note that opting out of personalized advertising does not mean you will no longer see advertisements. You will continue to see ads, but they may be less relevant to your interests.

Dynamic Advertising Consent Management & Revocation Architecture

Consent granted by you for telemetry collection and algorithmic ad personalization is not an irrevocable contract. We guarantee your sovereign right to revoke, limit, or modify your consent at any time with zero friction.

To modify your privacy preferences in real time, you can access the application's internal settings menu (e.g., navigating to [Settings > Privacy & Data Settings] within Prisma Noticias or FirmApp) and select "Manage Ad Consent Options". This interactive trigger instantly reinvokes the User Messaging Platform (UMP SDK) panel, allowing you to withdraw consent globally or configure granular preferences for specific analytics and advertising vendors listed in the IAB TCF ecosystem.

Additionally, you retain complete hardware-level control to halt advertising identifier transmission directly via your device operating system settings (App Tracking Transparency on iOS or Reset/Delete Advertising ID on Android).

Rights under CPRA and U.S. State Laws (Official "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" Notice)

In strict accordance with United States state privacy regulations, including the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) and the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA), the automated exchange of unique device identifiers (Ad IDs), IP addresses, and in-app behavioral telemetry with our advertising network partners (Google AdMob, AppLovin, Vungle) for cross-context behavioral advertising legally constitutes a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information.

Arandu Labs LLC receives no direct monetary compensation for this transfer, operating strictly within programmatic mediation ecosystems. Nonetheless, you hold the absolute right to opt out of this data dissemination.

To exercise your statutory right to "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information", you may utilize the in-app privacy controls or enable Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals transmitted by your browser/device. At the infrastructure level, Arandu Labs integrates support for the IAB Global Privacy Platform (GPP) and transmits Restricted Data Processing (RDP) signals to Google servers upon opt-out.
